2. Who This Policy Applies To

This policy covers two types of people who interact with Clock-Bot:

  • End Users — employees or crew members who use Clock-Bot to receive shift and break reminders. End users typically access Clock-Bot through a device provided or directed by their employer (the Business Customer).

3. What We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to deliver notifications.

a) Device Identifier
A randomly generated unique ID is created and stored locally on your device when you first open Clock-Bot. This ID is used to match your device to your notification schedule on our servers. It is not tied to your name, phone number, email address, or any other identifying information.

b) Notification Schedule
When you tap "Set Reminders," the times you selected (clock-in, lunch, dinner, end of shift) are sent to our servers along with your device identifier. We do not store your name, job title, employer name, or location.

c) Push Notification Token
To deliver notifications to Android devices, we store a push subscription token issued by your browser. This token is a technical identifier used only to route notifications and is not linked to any personal information.

d) ntfy Topic (iPhone Users Only)
iPhone users receive notifications via the ntfy.sh notification relay service. A topic code derived from your device identifier (e.g., qr-a1b2c3d4e5f6g7h8) is stored on our servers and transmitted to ntfy.sh solely for notification delivery.

What we do NOT collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any contact information
  • Your GPS location or physical address
  • Your actual clock-in or clock-out times from your employer's timekeeping system
  • Browsing history, cookies, or tracking pixels
  • Payment information (processed by third-party payment providers; we never see your card number)
  • Any data from your device beyond what is listed above

4. How We Use Your Data

We use collected data exclusively to:

  • Deliver push notifications to your device at your scheduled times
  • Store your schedule on our servers until notifications have fired or the 48-hour expiry is reached
  • Route notifications through the appropriate delivery channel (Web Push for Android, ntfy.sh for iPhone)
  • Diagnose technical issues when requested by you or your employer

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, behavioral analysis, or any purpose other than delivering the reminders you requested.

5. Data Retention

Schedule data (notification times, push tokens, device identifiers) is automatically and permanently deleted from our servers within 48 hours of being created — sooner if all scheduled notifications have already fired. We do not maintain archives or backups of deleted schedule data.

There is no "account" to delete because we never created one. Your device identifier exists only on your device (in local browser storage) and on our server for up to 48 hours.

6. Third-Party Services

Clock-Bot relies on the following third-party infrastructure to function. Each has its own privacy policy.

  • Cloudflare, Inc. — Hosts our notification scheduling server. Data is processed on Cloudflare's global network. Privacy policy: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy
  • ntfy.sh (Binwiederhier) — iPhone notification relay service. Your ntfy topic code is transmitted to ntfy.sh servers when a notification is sent. Privacy policy: ntfy.sh/privacy
  • Apple Inc. (APNs) — Apple's Push Notification Service is used to deliver notifications to iPhones through ntfy. Apple's privacy policy applies to this transmission.
  • Google LLC (FCM) — Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging is used to deliver notifications to Android devices. Google's privacy policy applies to this transmission.
  • GitHub, Inc. — The Clock-Bot web application is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect standard web server logs (IP address, browser type). Privacy policy: docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share your data with any third party beyond what is listed above and necessary for notification delivery.
