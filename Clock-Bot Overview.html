Internal Tool Overview

Clock-Bot
Crew Clock Reminder

An automatic notification system that reminds field crews exactly when to clock in and out for lunch and dinner — even when their phone is locked and in their pocket.

The Problem It Solves

Missed clock-ins cost money — on both sides

Field crews are busy. When it's time to clock out for lunch, they're often mid-task, and the reminder slips their mind. Late or missed punches mean payroll corrections, supervisor follow-ups, and potential labor compliance issues. Clock-Bot eliminates that by sending automatic, time-sensitive notifications directly to each crew member's phone — no apps to open, no timers to set.

How Crew Members Use It — 2 Minutes, Once a Day
1

Open Clock-Bot on their phone

It's a website saved to the home screen — looks and feels like an app, no App Store download required.

2

Enter their start time

Type in what time they clocked in (or will clock in) — e.g. 7:30 AM.

3

Pick their lunch hour mark

Choose when lunch should be — e.g. "4h 30m" means clock out 4.5 hours after start. The app shows the exact time automatically.

4

Tap "Set Reminders" — done

They can put their phone away. Clock-Bot handles the rest. Notifications arrive even if the phone is locked, the app is closed, or they have no signal at the time of setup.

5

Notification arrives on time

A heads-up pops up a few minutes before, followed by the actual clock-out reminder. They tap it, open ADP, and punch in/out.

Notifications the Crew Receives
🔔

Heads-Up

Arrives a few minutes before lunch — "Lunch in 5 min — heads up!" — so they can wrap up what they're doing.

🍽️

Clock Out

"Clock Out — Lunch Break" fires at the exact mark so they open ADP and punch out immediately.

✅

Clock Back In

After their break (30, 45, or 60 min), a reminder fires to clock back in before they forget.

🌙

Dinner (Long Days)

Same three notifications repeat for dinner break on long days. Can be turned off for short days.

Privacy & Data
🔒

Minimal data, no tracking

Clock-Bot stores only what's needed to fire notifications: a random device identifier and the scheduled notification times. No names, no location, no personal information. Schedules automatically delete after 48 hours. No data is sold or shared with any third party. The tool is internal-only.

Common Questions
Does every crew member need to create an account?
No. On Android there's zero account setup. On iPhone they install the free ntfy app and subscribe to one personal code — no registration, no email, no password.
What if a crew member's schedule changes day to day?
They open the app, adjust their start time and hour mark, and tap "Set Reminders" again. Takes under a minute. The previous schedule is automatically replaced.
Does it work if there's no cell service?
The schedule is saved to a cloud server when "Set Reminders" is tapped (requires a brief internet connection at setup time). After that, notifications are sent by the server — so even if the phone loses signal, as long as it comes back before the notification fires, it will be delivered.
Can it send reminders to the whole crew at once?
Each crew member sets their own start time since they may clock in at different times. Notifications are personalized per device. There's no "broadcast to all" — that's intentional, so each person gets the right time for their shift.
Does it require IT or a software vendor?
No. Clock-Bot is a lightweight web app built and maintained in-house. There's no vendor contract, no enterprise software license, and no IT infrastructure required beyond the two $5/month cloud services listed above.
